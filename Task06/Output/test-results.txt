TASK 06 – DEADLOCK AND INTER-THREAD COMMUNICATION

Test:
Producer generates 10 items.
Consumer processes all 10 items.
Buffer capacity: 5.

Result:
10 items produced.
10 items consumed.
All items processed successfully.

STATUS: PASSED

CONCEPTS:
1. wait() pauses a thread and releases the object's monitor.
2. notifyAll() wakes waiting threads.
3. while checks the condition again after waking.
4. synchronized protects shared buffer data.
5. Circular queue uses modulo operation.

DEADLOCK:
Deadlock occurs when threads wait indefinitely for locks held by each other.

PREVENTION:
- Acquire locks in a consistent order.
- Avoid unnecessary nested locks.
- Use tryLock() with timeout where appropriate.

ALTERNATIVE:
ArrayBlockingQueue provides built-in thread-safe producer-consumer operations.

COMPLEXITY:
Time: O(1) per put() or take().
Space: O(5) = O(1) for the fixed buffer.

CONCLUSION:
wait() and notifyAll() support safe communication between producer and consumer threads.
